01 · The two models

Two ways to close the loop between urban waste and local manufacturing

Both qualify as Circular Systemic Solutions: each closes a defined urban material loop inside a bounded territory and binds public authorities, waste operators, manufacturers and users into one governed configuration.

Shared Hub Model — territorial operator routes streams to many manufacturers
Integrated Manufacturer Model — one manufacturer owns the full circular loop
MODEL APilot 1

Shared Hub Model

A territorial operator establishes shared preparation infrastructure that classifies, certifies and routes urban secondary streams to local manufacturing demand — so no single manufacturer has to organise its own sourcing chain.

  • Intake, classification, certification, routing and uptake run as one chain
  • A manufacturer relies on the whole sequence without operating any part of it
  • Demonstrated on urban WEEE in the Alicante functional territory
MODEL BPilots 2 & 3

Integrated Manufacturer Model

A manufacturer organises a regional circular sourcing chain and reincorporates urban-derived secondary material and products directly into production — without relying on shared intermediary infrastructure.

  • Operational baseline, acceptance criteria, validation, market reintegration
  • Run against two different binding constraints to prove transfer conditions
  • Demonstrated on secondary EPS insulation (PL) and BEMS recovery (DE)

03 · The case

Challenges, results & impact

In 2023 the EU collected only 37.5% of its WEEE and recycled just 14.8 of every 35.3 kg of plastic packaging per capita. The material exists — what is missing is the operational system to upgrade it into competitive, locally usable manufacturing input.

SO1 · URBAN WEEE

No preparation-for-reuse capacity, and no certified route through which a manufacturer can accept a recovered component.

SO2 · POST-CONSUMER EPS

Recycling clean post-industrial regrind is routine. Qualifying post-consumer EPS — unknown contamination, moisture and legacy flame retardants — is not.

SO3 · BUILDING EQUIPMENT

Five conditions block reuse at once: cheap new kit, unknown history, sub-economic volumes, no warranty-equivalent recertification, no way to procure performance instead of ownership.

SO4 · DIGITAL & KNOWLEDGE

Hubs route devices without automated identification or records a manufacturer will accept; replicators lack data on where streams and demand coincide.

SO5 · COMPARABLE EVIDENCE

Circular claims are rarely paired or comparable, so the effect of choosing one governance model over another cannot be established.

SO6 · REPLICATION

Guidance is derived from single configurations, so a territory cannot tell which model its own collection density, manufacturing base and rules can support.

Binding targets by M48, verified at the production boundary under one indicator protocol fixed at M18 and reviewed to ISO 14071:2024.
